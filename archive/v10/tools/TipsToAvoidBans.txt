Botting ALWAYS has the risk of a ban
Well made AHK scripts and randomising recordings will help reduce bans but will not prevent bans themslves if you do not follow these guidelines

Appeal a ban link
https://support.runescape.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360000867958-Appeal-a-ban

Don't bot straight off Tutorial Island

Bot during the same hours you would play normally

Slowly increase the hours you play/bot
Do not suddenly bot a new activity for 10 hours

If botting a new activity or skill, start at less than 1 hour and slowly increase bot time to no more than 3-4 hours at a time

Don't bot for exact hour intervals, many botters using clients configure their bots this way and is an easy metric for Jagex to use

Don't do the same activity more than a few days in a row

Do a variety of activities e.g. quests, achievements, minigames, bosses

For the macro recorder specifically, the goal is to have more time in recordings than you bot for which will be basically fool proof. Play normally and record several minutes many times and keep adding recordings when you have time.

BAD EXAMPLES
Running Herb Cleaner bot overnight having never cleaned herbs on the account before
Only fletching bows for a week straight 10 hours a day

GOOD EXAMPLE
Botting crafting for quest requirements an hour a day between boss runs, completing the quest then changing activity to bot
